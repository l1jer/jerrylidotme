Our History

Founded in 2005, IT&A Bit Computing Solutions began as a small computer support service in Canberra. Over the years, we've grown into a comprehensive IT solutions provider serving businesses across Australia.

With a team of over 50 certified IT professionals, we've helped hundreds of businesses optimise their technology infrastructure, improve security posture, and leverage digital tools for growth.
